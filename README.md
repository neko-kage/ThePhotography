# The Photography

*A Minecraft mod about the history, technology, and art of photography.*

## Overview

**The Photography** is a Minecraft mod dedicated to bringing the world of photography into Minecraft. Inspired by the evolution of photographic technology, the project aims to transform photography from a simple way to capture screenshots into an immersive in-game craft.

From the mechanical ingenuity of early cameras to the possibilities of digital imaging, The Photography explores how cameras work, how photographs are made, and how photographic technology has evolved over time.

## Vision

The project aims to create a photography experience that combines historical authenticity, technical depth, and creative freedom.

- **Camera Systems** — Explore cameras with different designs, capabilities, and characteristics.
- **Lenses and Optics** — Experiment with focal lengths, fields of view, and different approaches to image composition.
- **Photographic History** — Discover the evolution of photography, from early photographic processes to modern digital imaging.
- **Film and Imaging** — Explore different photographic media, image formats, and imaging technologies.
- **Photo Collection** — Capture, view, preserve, and organize photographs of the Minecraft world.
- **Creative Expression** — Document adventures, landscapes, architecture, and the stories that unfold throughout the world.

These features represent the project's broader direction and may be developed progressively.

## Design Philosophy

Photography is more than pressing a button to save an image. It is a combination of optics, mechanics, chemistry, electronics, and artistic expression.

The Photography aims to reflect this diversity through a system that rewards curiosity and experimentation. Rather than treating every camera as an interchangeable screenshot tool, the project seeks to give photographic equipment its own identity and make the process of taking a photograph meaningful.

Historical inspiration and technical experimentation will guide the project's development, while Minecraft remains the space in which players can explore, create, and tell stories.

## Project Status

The Photography is an ongoing project. Its implementation and feature set will evolve over time as the underlying photography systems are developed.

Ideas described in this README represent the overall project vision and should not be interpreted as a list of currently implemented features.

## Compatibility

The current development target is:

- **Minecraft:** 1.21.1
- **Mod Loader:** NeoForge
- **Java:** 21

Compatibility may change as development progresses.
